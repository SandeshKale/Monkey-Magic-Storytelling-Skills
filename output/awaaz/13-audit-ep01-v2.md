## Lessons for next prompts
1. Phone-voice windows need the explicit line "lips pressed together and still for these seconds: ...". Parted-lip drift is the commonest failure.
2. Never write "gasp", "catches her breath" or "sob" for a silent beat; write "breathes through her nose, lips closed".
3. Ask for the same face in every shot by attaching only `CANON-` and removing extra identity words (headphones, clean-shaven).
4. For anything that must be exact, ask for one touch and one movement per hand, then "the hand returns to rest".
5. Grok will cut inside a 15 s clip if the action is long; keep each beat sequence short and ask for "one continuous take".
