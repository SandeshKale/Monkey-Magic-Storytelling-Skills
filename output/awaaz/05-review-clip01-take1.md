## Exact fixes and decisions
1. **Bindi.** She wears a small red bindi. My earlier look did not mention one and the script called her widowed. Decision: keep the take, drop "widowed" (the story does not use it), and add the bindi to the look lock. Done in all files.
2. **Saree and blouse.** She is in a sage-green saree over a deep teal blouse, not an all-teal saree. Adopted as canon. Done in all files.
3. **Trim to 5.0 s.** The last second adds nothing. Ending on the tears is a good cut into clip 2.
4. **"Kabir?" is 0.3 s late and loud.** The phone voice (voice session A, line 1) goes at 0.3 s and runs about 2 s, so it ends before the word starts. I normalise the word's level in the mix.
5. **Skin and detail.** A little painterly at 400 px, as expected. After upscaling to 1080x1920 with light sharpening and grain it holds up on a phone screen. A sample is in `output/assembly/awaaz-ep01-clip01_trim5s_upscaled.mp4`.
