# Intake sheet

Fill what you can. Leave the rest blank and the package will mark each gap as GAP instead of guessing.

## Core (the story cannot be written without these)

1. What happened, or what will happen? (facts only, in your own words)
   Has it already happened?  yes / not yet

2. What is the goal, and what breaks if it fails?

3. Who or what stands in the way? (person, system, weather, body, object, yourself)

4. Why are you making this, beyond views?

5. What did you believe at the start? What do you believe now? (If you have not shot it yet: what belief do you expect might move?)

## Always

6. What will you not show or fake?
