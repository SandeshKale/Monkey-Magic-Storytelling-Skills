# 06 Rasa theory

From the Natyashastra. Nine performance emotions (Navras). A story that rides one emotion the whole way becomes noise. A good piece moves through several.

You do not force all nine. Four to six is the YouTube target.

## The nine (keep Sanskrit keys)

| Key | Gloss | YouTube cue |
|---|---|---|
| Hasya | Humour | A batting clown, a wrong seat, a dry aside |
| Raudra | Anger | Injustice named on camera |
| Vibhatsa | Disgust | Cruelty or filth the character must face |
| Bhayanaka | Fear | What happens if the goal fails |
| Shringara | Delight / attraction | Beauty, song, crush on a place or person |
| Vira | Courage | The moment they accept the fight |
| Karuna | Pity / compassion | A child, a stranger, the self in trouble |
| Adbhuta | Wonder | A fact that rearranges the map |
| Shanta | Calm | Silence after the peak, a river, a completed vow |

Lagaan carries almost all of these. That is why it is used as the teaching film. Your 12-minute vlog will not. Pick a dominant rasa and three supporting ones.

## EEI mix

YouTube videos that last tend to mix

- Entertainment
- Education
- Inspiration

MrBeast skews entertainment + inspiration. A train film that also teaches how a route or a community works adds education. Pure lecture without entertainment is a class, not a video.

If the creator feels the emotion while recording, the audience can feel it. Calculated rasa with a dead face reads as fake.

## Script rule

On the beat sheet, tag each beat with one rasa key. If the same key repeats for five beats, insert a contrasting rasa or cut.
