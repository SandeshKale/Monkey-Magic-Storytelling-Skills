# Script templates

Fill brackets with the user's true material. Delete any line you cannot make true.

## Long-form hybrid (12–15 min)

```
TITLE
[dramatic idea, concrete noun, implied duration or scale]

THUMBNAIL
[one visible mismatch]

SPINE
When [character] wants [goal] but [antagonist] stands in the way, they [struggle], and leave [changed].

WHY (spoken by 0:45)
[one personal sentence that is not about views]

COLD OPEN (0–20s, visuals)
[pictures of the mismatch or the machine already moving]
[music or location sound, few or no words]

INCITE + GOAL
[one sentence idle world]
[inciting incident]
[goal]
[stakes]

CONFLICT 1
[mismatch]
[what they try]
[what breaks]

CONFLICT 2
[new problem, new rasa]

CONFLICT 3
[point of no return decision]

HUMAN BEAT
[one true vulnerability]

CLIMAX
[attempt]
[peak rasa]

CHANGE
[start belief versus end belief, shown not lectured]

CUT
[last image]
[end screen only after cut]
```

## Title pack

Write 5 titles. Each must

- lead with the idea, not the word "vlog"
- contain one concrete noun
- imply scale, duration, or mismatch
- be payable by the footage

Examples of shape, not to copy — "4 days on India's longest train" / "I paid $0 electricity for a year" / "Will a cheap extender save a dead PG Wi-Fi?"

## Thumbnail brief

One sentence — who or what is in the wrong place, and what the face or object is doing. No collage of five promises.
