# Script templates

Fill brackets with the user's true material. Delete any line you cannot make true, or leave it as `[GAP: …]`. Tag beats with `F#`, `STAGE`, or `GAP`.

## Long-form hybrid (12–15 min)

```

[defaults you filled in: format, language, runtime]

TITLE
[dramatic idea, concrete noun, implied duration or scale]

THUMBNAIL
[one visible mismatch]

When [character] wants [goal] but [antagonist] stands in the way, they [struggle], and leave [changed].

WHY (spoken by 0:45, aim 0:30)   Src:
[one personal sentence that is not about views]

COLD OPEN (0–20s, visuals)   Src:
[pictures of the mismatch or the machine already moving]
[music or location sound, few or no words]

[inciting incident]
```

## Prospective shoot plan

Use when the event has not happened yet.

```
MODE
prospective. Nothing below has happened.

SPINE
When [character] wants [goal] but [antagonist] stands in the way, they [plan],
and the belief to watch for is [start belief → ?].

STAGED BEATS (each needs creator approval)
1. [scene]   Src: STAGE   Safety: [what could go wrong / safeguard]
2. [scene]   Src: STAGE   Safety: [ … ]

CAPTURE LIST
[shots, sounds, people to ask for consent]

ENDING PROMPT
[GAP: creator's honest reflection after the shoot]
Questions to answer on camera: What did you believe when you started? What do you believe now?

TITLE (names the challenge, not the outcome)
[ … ]

OPEN ITEMS
[approvals, permissions, disclosures]
```

## Title pack

Write 3–5 titles, 100 characters or fewer. Each must

- lead with the idea, not the word "vlog"
- contain one concrete noun
- imply scale, duration, or mismatch
- be payable by the footage, and in prospective mode claim no outcome

Examples of shape, not to copy — "4 days on India's longest train" / "I paid $0 electricity for a year" / "Will a cheap extender save a dead PG Wi-Fi?"

## Thumbnail brief

One sentence — who or what is in the wrong place, and what the face or object is doing. No collage of five promises.
