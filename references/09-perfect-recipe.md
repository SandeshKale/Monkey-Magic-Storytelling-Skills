# 09 Perfect recipe for a YouTube video

There is no perfect formula for art. There is a recipe that keeps working when the ingredients are true.

A good YouTube video, in platform terms, has CTR + retention + engagement. If those are missing, the video is not good enough. Do not blame the algorithm first.

## Recipe

1. Start from real drama already in the life or the idea. Amplify placement, not fiction.
2. Title is idea-first and dramatic. "India's longest train journey" beats "vlog 47."
3. Thumbnail shows the same drama the title names.
4. Open on pictures and sound for a few seconds. Let the title's curiosity work before the mouth does.
5. Speak the why. Correct a wrong assumption if you have one (the longest route is not the one they think).
6. Jump into action. First conflict within the first two minutes.
7. Stack 3–6 conflicts. Each one is a mismatch or a blocked goal.
8. Move from idea to person. Share a vulnerability once the viewer is already on the train.
9. Build an awkward beat, then let kindness or competence resolve it (catharsis). Replay the peak if it earned it.
10. Show the transformation in a concrete image (pizza eaten alone, then shared). It must be real footage or a clearly labeled reenactment.
11. End on an honest line. Short. Then end screen.

## Hybrid path (default)

Minute 0–3 live in the idea. Minute 3 onward let the person take the frame. Viewers who clicked for a train stay for a human.

## Length

A 15–18 minute hybrid can hold six conflicts. A 7-minute video can hold three. Do not pad. See the tiers in the canonical numbers table.

## Script rule

If the middle has only travel footage and no new problem, it is a slideshow. Insert the next true conflict or cut runtime.
