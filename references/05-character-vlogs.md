# 05 Character and why some daily vlogs work

Daily vlogs work only when the audience has a relationship with the character. Routine is not a relationship. Honesty is.

## How attachment forms

- Time on screen with one person.
- Honesty about flaws. No human is perfect. Showing the shortcoming is the shortest path to relatability.
- Peak emotion that is real — a dance in the kitchen, a cry that is not performed. Faked crying ends the story.
- A public goal the viewer can track across episodes (become Hokage, reach London, wire the village).
- An arc. The person in episode 30 is not the person in episode 1.

Sourav Joshi-style family vlogs work because the audience adopted the family. Cloned MrBeast challenges fail because there is no adopted person.

## 70 / 30 again

In a vlog series, the idea of "today we went to X" is 30. The person walking through X is 70.

## New-creator problem

A new channel has no earned attachment yet. Do not wait ten years. Earn the first attachment by

1. One honest vulnerability in video one.
2. One named long goal the series will chase.
3. One mismatch per episode so the day is not a routine.

## Script rule

For a vlog, ask the creator once for up to three shortcomings they are genuinely willing to show. They choose. Never press, and never suggest a trauma. Use one. Never invent one, and never use someone else's.
