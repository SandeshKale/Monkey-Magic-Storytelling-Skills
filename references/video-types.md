# Video types

Pick one before writing.

## Idea-based

The click is the premise. World's largest X. Longest Y. $1 versus $1M. Character can be thin at the click and must thicken in the body or the video feels like a stunt channel.

## Personalized vlog

The click is the person. Works while real drama exists in the life (a fight at work, studying law late, a family in motion). Dies when the life becomes a loop of branded mornings.

## Hybrid (default)

Title sells the idea. Body delivers the person. Best default for growing channels and for LLM-generated packages, because it does not require a pre-existing parasocial bond and does not hide the human.

## Series versus one-off

A series needs a public long goal and a visible arc across episodes. Each episode still needs its own change. "Day 47 of 100" is a container, not a story.

## Process versus story

Process shows steps. Story shows why the steps cost something. Add Y and mismatch to a process video rather than replacing the process.

## Object-led

The product or place is treated as a character in the wrong room. Still needs a human voice with a Y.
