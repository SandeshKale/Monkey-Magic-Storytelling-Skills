# 07 Three-act structure

Stories already have this shape. People who cannot tell a story are people who wander off the shape.

## The three acts

### Act 1 — Beginning

Character sits in an idle world (equilibrium). An inciting incident knocks them out of it.

Idle world on YouTube is one sentence, not twenty minutes of cupboard tours.

Inciting incident examples — the business is wiped out, the letter arrives, Covid shuts the shop, the ticket is booked, the helmet-less crash is seen.

### Act 2 — Middle

Point of no return. A decision is taken that cannot be undone. Team is assembled. Training starts. The village tells the king they will play.

Conflicts stack here. This is most of a YouTube runtime.

### Climax (bridge of Act 2 into Act 3)

Emotions peak. Do or die. In Lagaan the cricket match is a long climax. On YouTube the climax is the attempt itself — the last station, the last test, the apology.

### Act 3 — End

Resolution. The character returns to a world, changed. Bhuvan is a hero. The rider now owns a helmet.

## YouTube mutation

YouTube videos often have no true "back home" ending. Walking the character to their apartment after the vow is complete is undramatic. Cut at the payoff — the bus door, the bill that reads zero, the helmet order confirmation.

Do not fabricate extra tension after the change is visible.

## Time map for a 12–15 min video

| Clock | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Cold open. Pictures and sound, at most one spoken sentence |
| 0:20–0:45 | Inciting incident + why (Why lands by 0:45, aim for 0:30) |
| 0:45–2:00 | Goal, stakes, first mismatch. First conflict by 2:00 |
| 2:00–10:00 | Stacked conflicts, rasa shifts, point of no return |
| 10:00–13:00 | Climax |
| 13:00–14:30 | Change visible, honest line, cut |
| last 20s | End screen only after the story is over |

The opening contract (cold open, Why by 0:45, first conflict by 2:00) stays fixed at any runtime. Shorter videos compress the middle of the same spine. They do not delete the change.
