# 11 YouTube Shorts

Shorts are not long-form chopped at 60 seconds. They are a full story with no idle world.

## Job

Discovery. High retention. A door into the long-form on the same channel. A Short that lives on a different personality than the main videos trains the wrong audience.

Target intended retention 75–80 percent or better. This is the course's heuristic, not a platform guarantee. Views follow retention on this surface.

## Spine

1. Hook in the first second — rivalry, wrong object, interrupted expectation. Not "I am bored."
2. One goal, one antagonist, one question that cannot be answered until the last seconds.
3. Progressive reveal. Third place, then second, then first. Slow result first.
4. One human crumb (a sip of water, a wait, a grin) so there is a person, not only a premise.
5. Payoff + optional mid-wait subscribe line that does not break the loop.
6. Cut on the answer. No ride home.
7. No new facts. Everything in the Short appears in the long-form or the user's ledger. Spoken lines stay at 12 words or fewer.

## Devices that hold a loop

- Two characters arguing a binary (this app vs that app) and a clock.
- An object that does not belong (a vegetable in a tiffin).
- A test with a feared outcome.
- A countdown the viewer wants to beat.

Keep the tone light. Banter, not trauma, unless the long-form already lives in that register and the Short is a true sibling.

## Length

Long enough to complete the question. Short enough that a loop feels cheap to rewatch. Do not pad with a second question.

## Script rule

Write the last line first (the answer). Then write the first line as the question that makes that answer necessary. Everything between those two lines must delay the answer without changing the subject.
