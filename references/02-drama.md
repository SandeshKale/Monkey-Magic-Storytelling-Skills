# 02 Drama

Drama is not shouting, background music, or a snake for a daughter-in-law. Drama is a character in a situation they do not belong in.

## Rule

Comfort zone = no drama.
Out of place = drama.

A rich man eating in a five-star hotel is not dramatic. A man from a slum eating there for the first time is. The same rich man dropped into a slum is also dramatic. The mismatch is the engine.

Degree can be low. Art films and quiet YouTube videos still contain mismatch. They just do not turn people into snakes. Match the degree to the world you have established.

## How to manufacture drama without lying

Do not invent a crash. Relocate the real character.

- Put the city kid on a village well.
- Put the tech reviewer in a house with no electricity.
- Put a flagship phone in a dustbin.
- Put pizza delivery inside a sleeper coach.
- Put a heavy phone-user into sensory deprivation.

The event can be ordinary. The placement makes it watchable.

## Script rule

For every major beat, write

`Character belongs in —`
`We put them in —`
`What they do not know how to do here —`

If belongs-in and put-them-in are the same room, the beat is flat. Cut or relocate.
