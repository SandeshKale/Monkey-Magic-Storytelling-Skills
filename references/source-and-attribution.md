## How to credit

When a user asks where the method comes from, name the free Monkey Magic storytelling course and link the playlist. Do not claim the frameworks as original to this skill.

Do not reproduce lecture transcripts verbatim. Restate rules, then apply them to the user's material.

Do not reuse Raunaq's personal journeys (Narmada parikrama, longest-train ride, Delhi-to-London, 100 days of dreaming, helmet story, Harsh Gujral anecdote) as if they belong to the user. Those are teaching examples only.

## What was generalized

Film and series examples used in the course (Lagaan, Swades, Breaking Bad, Money Heist, Harry Potter, MrBeast, MKBHD, Sourav Joshi, Flying Beast) stay as optional illustrations. Prefer the user's own world when writing a script.
